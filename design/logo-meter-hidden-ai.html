Wider gap → reads as A · I.

The short + tall bars stay, but the spacing is tuned so the silhouette reads as two letters. Still a meter first, letters second.

02 serifed · lowercase a · i

The dot becomes the i.

A slim top serif on the short bar makes it read as lowercase "a". On the tall bar, the accent cap reads as the tittle on "i". Still meter-first; letters emerge at certain sizes.

Subtlety: ★★★★ — warmer, more human. Plays well at type-scale.

The short bar gets a crossbar.

A horizontal notch across the middle of the short bar is the crossbar of capital A. The tall bar stays plain I. Overt to anyone looking for it; invisible at favicon sizes.

04 triangular · A silhouette

The short bar becomes a triangle.

Slanting the short bar's top edge turns it into a proper A silhouette. Reads as "A · I" very clearly — and also as a tiny mountain rising beside a tower. Most overt.

Subtlety: ★★ — the one everyone will notice. Use if you want AI up-front.
