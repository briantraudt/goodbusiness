01 the grin

A face hiding in the wordmark.

The "oo" in Good are eyes. A confident crescent smile sits under the word. Reads as a wordmark at a glance; as a face when you look twice. Gets away with coral because the smile earns it.

03 the portal

A cut. Through anything.

One filled circle with a diagonal slice cut through it, off-center. Reads as an aperture, a doorway, a ringed planet, a cursor through space. The slice is the brand device — it recurs in dividers, crops, photography. Simple to own, weird enough to be remembered.
